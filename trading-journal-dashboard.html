📋 Trading Plan & Rules
Last updated: Jun 1, 2025 · Version 3.2 · Read every morning
⚠️ Read This Every Morning Before Trading
Your trading plan is your constitution. Every deviation is a vote against your future self. If a trade isn't in the plan — don't take it. Consistency beats perfection every time.
Core Trading Rules
01Never risk more than 1% of account per trade
02Max 3 open positions at any time
03Stop trading after 2 consecutive losses — 30 min break minimum
04Daily max loss: -$500. Hit it → trading day is over. No exceptions.
05Only trade high-quality setups — patience is a position
06Never move stop loss to increase risk — only to lock profits
07Minimum R:R must be 2:1 before entry
08No trading first 15 minutes of open — observe only
09No news trades unless pre-planned and sized down 50%
10Log EVERY trade. No exceptions. Ever.

  Trading Hours & Sessions
Prime Session: 9:45 AM – 11:30 AM EST — highest quality setups, most follow-through
Afternoon Session: 2:00 PM – 3:30 PM EST — trend continuation only, half size
Avoid: 12:00 PM – 1:30 PM EST (lunch chop), last 30 min (erratic fills)
  Timeframe Hierarchy
Top-down approach every morning:
Daily chart → bias & key support/resistance levels
1H chart → structure, trend direction, major S/R
5-min chart → entry timing, pattern confirmation
1-min chart → precision entry only (never for analysis)
  Mental Framework & Mindset Rules
Every trade is a probability, not a certainty. You are playing the long game across hundreds of executions.

Before every trade ask: "Would I be comfortable if 100 traders took this exact same trade right now?"

Losses are tuition. Log them, learn from them, move forward with discipline.

Trigger Log
FOMO  Missing a big move causes me to chase
Trigger: Watching a stock run without me after passing on the setup.
Pattern: I enter late, chasing price near a short-term top.
Fix: Write "There will always be another trade" pre-market. Keep watchlist loaded with quality setups so I'm never desperate.
REVENGE  After a loss I want to win it back
Trigger: Hitting stop on a confident trade.
Pattern: Immediately looking for next trade, sizing up.
Fix: Mandatory 30-min break after any loss. Log the trade immediately while emotions are fresh.
OVERCONFIDENCE  Winning streak makes me size too large
Trigger: Multiple consecutive wins creating an "invincible" feeling.
Fix: Position size is ALWAYS calculated from the formula — never gut feeling. Spreadsheet check before every entry.
Win Rate by Emotional State

📖 Setup Guide

Quick Start — 3 Steps
Step 1: Duplicate template → Step 2: Fill in your Trading Plan & Rules → Step 3: Log your first trade in Daily Log. You're up and running today.
1  How to Duplicate This Template
1. Open the template link while logged into Notion.
2. Click "Duplicate" button in the top-right corner.
3. Choose your workspace from the dropdown.
4. Wait 30–60 seconds for all pages and databases to copy.
5. Template appears in sidebar as "TradingOS — Trading Journal".

Important: Don't share your duplicated copy publicly — this shares your personal trade data.
2  Personalizing Your Trading Plan
1. Navigate to "📋 Trading Plan & Rules".
2. Replace all placeholder text with YOUR actual rules.
3. Update Risk Parameters table with your account size.
4. Edit Allowed Setups to match your specific strategy.
5. Read your plan every morning before markets open.

Pro tip: Screenshot your rules and set it as your phone wallpaper.
3  Setting Up the Trade Database
1. Go to "🗃️ Master Trade Database".
2. Pre-configured with all properties and formulas — nothing to set up.
3. Click "+ New" to add your first trade.
4. Fill in: Date, Symbol, Direction, Setup, Entry/Exit, Stop, Target.
5. Formulas auto-calculate R-Multiple, P&L%, and R:R Ratio.
6. Upload chart screenshot to the Files property.

Note: Dashboard stats pull from this database. Log every trade here for accurate metrics.
4  Daily Workflow — How to Use Every Day
Pre-Market (30 min before open):
→ Duplicate Daily Log template for today's date
→ Complete mindset check (mood, sleep, energy)
→ Write pre-market notes and game plan

During Trading:
→ Log each trade immediately after execution
→ Upload chart screenshots right after each trade closes
→ Note emotional state during each trade

Post-Market (within 60 min of close):
→ Complete daily summary section
→ Answer the 3 reflection questions honestly
→ Add trades to the Master Trade Database
5  Connecting Live Charts (Google Sheets / TradingView)
Method 1 — Google Sheets (Recommended):
1. Export Notion database as CSV weekly
2. Import into Google Sheets → auto-generates charts

4. Embed URL in Notion using /embed block

Method 2 — TradingView:
